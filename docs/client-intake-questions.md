    - If no, do you want one designed?
18. **Do you have specific brand colors?** If not, we'll pick something that matches your signage.
19. **Any tagline or slogan you use?**
20. **Photos we could use on the site** — send whatever you've got:
    - The lot / storefront (exterior)
    - Your sign
    - Inside the office
    - You and/or your staff
    - *These matter more than you'd think. A site with real photos of a real place converts better than one with only car pictures — buyers are deciding whether you're legit.*
