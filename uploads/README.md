# Put photos here

Upload car photos into this folder from your phone: open the repo on
**github.com**, tap **Add file → Upload files**, and pick the pictures.

Then tell Claude which car they belong to — "those 8 photos are the silver
Cherokee" — and it will resize them, strip the location data your phone
embeds, put them on the car's page, and empty this folder again.

Nothing in here is part of the website. It is a drop box.
